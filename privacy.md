# CouchRally privacy

Updated 30 September 2026. Applies to CouchRally for Apple TV.

[English](privacy.md) · [Español](es/privacy.md) · [Norsk bokmål](nb/privacy.md)

## In the app

CouchRally does not require an app account and contains no advertising, analytics SDK or tracking code. The app does not send gameplay, card choices or scores to the developer. Language and sound preferences are stored locally on the Apple TV. Scores and the current game remain in that game session and are not saved as a player history.

## Purchases

Apple handles the optional Full Game purchase. CouchRally uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether the Apple Account has a verified right to the content. The app does not receive your card details and does not send purchase receipts to a developer server. Apple processes purchases and related information under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support

If you email support, Lars Ask receives the information you choose to send and uses it to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Please include only what is needed. Do not send account passwords, payment details or full purchase receipts. You can use the same contact address to ask about a support message or request its deletion. This support communication is separate from the app's gameplay.

## GitHub documentation

When you view these documents on GitHub, your visit is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). GitHub may process account information and technical visit data. If GitHub Pages is enabled, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. The documents add no analytics or tracking scripts.
