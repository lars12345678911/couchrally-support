# CouchRally support

CouchRally is for Apple TV with tvOS 17 or later. Four or more people can play using one Siri Remote; no phones or app accounts are needed.

[English](support.md) · [Español](es/support.md) · [Norsk bokmål](nb/support.md)

## Start a game

Choose Party Mix or one of the three game modes. Split into two to four teams, with at least two people on each team. Give the Siri Remote to a judge from another team. Each team plays three turns.

For Word Sprint and Mime Time, guessers face away from the TV. The clue-giver or performer and the judge can see the card. Word Sprint and Mime Time turns last 45 seconds. In Rapid Five, everyone may see the category: name five different things in 30 seconds. The group judges the answers.

In Word Sprint, do not say the answer, any part of it, or the three forbidden words; spelling and rhyming clues are not allowed. In Mime Time, use gestures without words, sounds, spelling or pointing at objects in the room. Each correct guess scores one point, and you may skip cards without losing points. In Rapid Five, five accepted answers score one point; an incomplete answer scores zero. Completing or skipping the category ends that turn.

## Pause, sound and language

Use the on-screen pause button or Play/Pause on the remote during a turn. Back/Menu also pauses an active turn. Pause hides the card and stops the timer; resume when everyone is ready. Use the language/rules screen to choose among 14 languages and turn game sounds on or off.

## What is free?

All three game modes include a free selection: 30 card ideas in total. The optional Full Game purchase adds 60 card ideas, for 90 in total. Every card is available in all 14 languages. The translations are not additional card ideas. Full Game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The app shows Apple's current price before purchase.

## Restore Full Game

Open the app's store and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase and connect the Apple TV to the internet. You do not need to buy Full Game again for that account. If it remains unavailable, contact support with the app version, tvOS version and the status shown on screen. Please do not send your password, payment details or full receipt.

If a purchase is waiting for approval, keep using the free cards while Apple processes it. The extra cards unlock only after the purchase is approved and verified. A cancelled purchase does not unlock cards. A refunded or revoked purchase no longer provides access to the extra cards.

## Offline play

The cards are included in the app. Free play works without an internet connection. Loading purchase information and restoring purchases can require internet access. After reinstalling the app, connect to the internet and restore your purchase if needed.

## Report a problem

Email [lars.ask@outlook.com](mailto:lars.ask@outlook.com). Include the Apple TV model, tvOS version, app version, selected language and the steps that caused the problem. For an unclear card, include its text and game mode. Screenshots should not show account or payment details.

Apple handles payments and refund requests: [request a refund from Apple](https://support.apple.com/118223).
